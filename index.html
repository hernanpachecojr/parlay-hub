How these are built. Leg prices are Polymarket's current ask for that side. The payout is 1 ÷ (product of leg prices), which assumes the legs are independent; Polymarket prices its own Combos, so the real payout can differ a little.
Stats estimate comes from ESPN season numbers: point or goal margins for team picks, and how often a player has cleared the line for props. A leg only makes a ticket when the stats agree with the side. Early-season samples are small and the market price already reflects public stats, so treat the estimate as context, not an edge.
